MFC Youth Area Management System
Public Beta v1.0.1

Thank you for trying the MFC Youth Area Management System.

This software is currently in Public Beta and is intended for testing and evaluation.

Please note:

• Some features may still be under development.
• Bugs or unexpected behavior may occur.
• Your feedback is greatly appreciated and will help improve future releases.

This application is an offline desktop system designed to help Missionary Families of Christ (MFC) Youth areas manage members, chapters, services, and activities through a centralized local database.

If you encounter any issues or would like to suggest improvements, please visit:
